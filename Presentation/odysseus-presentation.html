02 Our solution

Car state predicts, the map corrects

A causal filter: odometry bridges 0.1 s, cameras give absolute fixes, and weak fixes are trusted less.

Offline

3D map

SuperPoint on all training frames, triangulated with training poses. 20 M points cached on GPU.

Predict

Car-state EKF

Speed, vx, vy and yaw rate. ~2 cm drift per frame, so no visual odometry.

Correct

Guided matching + PnP

Project map points with the predicted pose, match within ±24 px, solve one 6-DoF PnP over all 7 cameras.

Weigh · Exp23

Selective trust

Fixes with few inliers or far from mapped lines get less weight; the car state carries the pose there.

03 Engineering decisions

What we dropped, and why this works

✕ Didn't work

  • Learned SLAM (MASt3R-SLAM, VGGT-Long): lost track or 313 m error. 4 m between frames at night is too much.
  • Visual odometry: best camera-only VO 43 m over 2 laps, vs 20.5 m from car state alone.
  • Track-border alignment: cameras sit 0.4 m high, so 0.5 m sideways moves the border ~1 px.
  • Trusting odometry everywhere: global down-weighting of fixes doubled the error on L5.

✓ Why we chose this

  • Car state for motion: 4× more accurate than VO over 1–10 s. Cameras do what odometry can't: absolute fixes.
  • Prediction makes matching cheap: guided search replaces retrieval, 410 → 41 ms.
  • All 7 cameras in one PnP: with one camera, yaw and sideways shift blur together (0.5 → 0.12 m).
  • Down-weight only weak fixes: keeps accuracy where the map is good, helps where it isn't.
Key insight The floor is the ground truth, not the matcher: every parameter sweep lands at 0.103–0.108 m, next to the GT's own ~0.1 m.
